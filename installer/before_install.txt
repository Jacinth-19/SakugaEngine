Sakuga Engine - what you need
=============================

Requirements
  * Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  * A Vulkan 1.3 capable GPU and its driver (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel). The Vulkan loader
    (vulkan-1.dll) is installed with the graphics driver - it is not part of this setup.
  * ~50 MB on disk for the app, its sample assets and its shaders.

What is included
  * sandbox.exe - the engine sandbox: motion lab with the hero avatar and NPCs, motion
    matching, the material inspector and the whole debug UI.
  * assets\  - sample models, LUTs, two HDRIs, and two preview mocap clips.
  * config\  - input actions and ui_theme.json (the ImGui look; F12 in the app edits it live).
  * shaders\ - precompiled SPIR-V, loaded at start-up.

The full LAFAN1 mocap library (77 clips, 333 MB) is not redistributed with this setup
because of its CC BY-NC-ND licence. Inside the app's folder, run

    tools\fetch_assets.sh --motion-only

from a bash shell (Git for Windows) to download it to %USERPROFILE%\.cache\lafan1, where
the sandbox picks it up automatically, or point it at a folder with --motion-dir DIR.
