Sakuga Engine - what you need
=============================

Requirements
  * Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  * A Vulkan 1.3 capable GPU and its driver (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel). The Vulkan loader
    (vulkan-1.dll) is installed with the graphics driver - it is not part of this setup.
  * At least 1 GB of free disk space for the application, extracted clips and motion cache.
  * The LAFAN1 animation dataset is licensed for non-commercial use only.
What is included
  * sandbox.exe - the engine sandbox: motion lab with the hero avatar and NPCs, motion
    matching, the material inspector and the whole debug UI.
  * assets\  - sample models, LUTs, two HDRIs, and 77 LAFAN1 motion clips and their license notices.
  * config\  - input actions and ui_theme.json (the ImGui look; F12 in the app edits it live).
  * shaders\ - precompiled SPIR-V, loaded at start-up.

The setup preview images are crops of earlier in-engine screenshots; their capture environment
was not recorded. The sample avatar is by pixiv Inc. (VRM 1.0 license). Appearance and performance
on your device may differ. See docs/media/README.md in the source repository for provenance.
